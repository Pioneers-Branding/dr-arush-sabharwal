Dr. Arush Sabharwal

MBBS, MS, FMAS, DMAS (Strasburg University, France)

Dr. Arush Sabharwal is the founder and chairman of Surgical Center for Obesity and Diabetes (SCOD). Currently SCOD has four clinics in Delhi, NCR and towards expansion in the near future. Dr. Sabharwal, holding a vast experience in surgery is associated with Jeewan Mala Hospital, Apollo Spectra Hospital (Karol Bagh), Foris C-Doc Hospital, Nehru Place and SCOD Clinic, Vasant Vihar as Chief of Bariatric and Metabolic Surgery.

His vision is to have pan India exclusive state of art centers for Bariatric and Metabolic Surgery. He feels that not only Obesity but various other metabolic problems like Diabetes, Infertility, Joints Pain etc are major epidemic which not only in india but the entire world is suffering.

India has already become the Diabetes Capital of the world and he wishes to contribute to the society in eradication of these problems from his country.
